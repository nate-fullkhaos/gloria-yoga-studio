Welcome

Supported Practice. Deeper Expression.

At PsyYogshala, we believe props aren't for show — they are tools of mastery for every level. We actively encourage our students to integrate props to achieve anatomical safety, flawless alignment, and a significantly deeper physical and mental experience in every posture.

You don’t need to pack a heavy bag to come to class. Our boutique studio is fully provisioned with premium, clean, and meticulously curated gear waiting for you at your mat:

  • Blocks & Straps For personal customization, reach, and stability.
  • Supportive Bolsters To open up the chest, support the spine, and facilitate restorative release.
  • Yoga Wheels Specifically utilized to safely open the shoulders and deepen backbends.
  • Iyengar Ropes Mounted securely to the walls to help you experience passive traction, alleviate spinal compression, and explore advanced inversions safely.
  • Premium Studio Mats Reserved for you every single time you book.
  • Yoga Chairs Used to support with balance, deepen stretches and chair yoga for seniors

Our practice

Every class combines traditional yoga with intelligent movement.

  • 🧘🏽‍♀️ Traditional Hatha Flow The root of your practice—classic postures and steady pacing found in your morning flows.
  • 💪🏽 Dumbbell Strength Integration Reflecting your unique 8 PM Strength Flow that blends resistance with yoga.
  • 🧱 Prop-Supported Alignment Highlighting your use of blocks, straps, and chairs for a safer, deeper practice.
  • 🪜 Iyengar Rope Tractions Showcasing your physical studio's unique wall ropes for decompression.
  • ☀️ Sunrise Sun Salutations Honoring the morning ritual, flow, and energy of your 6 AM class.
  • 🌬️ Pranayama & Breathwork Authentic breath control rooted in your Patanjali Rishikesh foundation.
  • 🌿 Mindful Self-Care & Pacing Creating space to pause and recharge, specifically curated for mothers and busy professionals.
  • 🔄 Mobility & Joint Stability The “intelligent movement” element ensuring you build strength without losing flexibility.
  • 🕊️ Deep Conscious Relaxation The essential wind-down at the end of every flow to reset your nervous system.
